Saved Files

    Pin to Home Screen: open a saved file, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The shortcut reopens that file directly, with its own icon.

    Saved files live in Safari's storage for this site, on this device only. If you don't open this app for a few weeks, iOS may clear them — just save the file again. For small files, Link copies a self-contained URL that keeps working regardless.

    Security Notice: Your HTML files stay on your device and are processed entirely within your browser, ensuring your privacy and security are fully protected. Nothing is ever uploaded.
