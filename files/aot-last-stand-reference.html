Attack on Titan

The Last Stand

Two people play two different games at the same table. The Titan player plays cards and hides one of them. The Hero players roll dice and have to guess what the hidden card does. Every re-roll the Heroes make can hand the Titan more power.

Titan — one player

Break the city

  • Destroy all 6 cannons, or
  • Eat all 12 citizens, or
  • Take the last heart from any one Hero

Any one of these ends the game

Heroes — one to four players

Cut the nape

  • Drive the Titan's health into the Kill Zone
  • Then meet every condition on Neck Slash or Eradication in one round

There is no other way for the Heroes to win

Setup

Build the tower and the Titan first. The rest takes two minutes.

  • Build the board. Assemble the tower. Put the 6 cannon pieces on it. Assemble the Titan standee. Slide the 6 platforms into it.
  • Set the trackers. Put the health marker at the start of the Titan health track. Put the 12 citizen tokens at the Titan's feet.
  • Titan player. Choose 1 of the 4 Titan cards. Take only the 7 action cards that match it. Return the rest to the box.
  • Each Hero player. Choose 1 of the 8 Hero standees and a coloured base. Take the matching Hero card. Take 3 heart tokens. Take the 5 dice in your base colour.
  • Place the Heroes. Each Hero starts on the ground (level 0) or on the tower (level 3 or level 5).
  • Tactics deck. Shuffle the 7 tactics cards. Stack them face up. Only the top card is available.

The round

Seven steps, in fixed order. Tap a step for the detail.

Play one card face up. Play the second card face down.

The face-up card is the Heroes' only warning. The face-down card is the bet they have to price.

A card played this round is set aside and cannot be played next round. It comes back the round after. The Heroes can track this.

All Heroes roll their 5 dice at the same time.

Every die showing the Titan face goes to the Titan player straight away.

Heroes may then re-roll any remaining dice as often as they want. Roll one die or several. Pause to talk whenever you like.

Every new Titan face also goes across. Re-rolling improves your hand and arms your opponent. That trade is the whole game.

Each ability on the Titan card costs a set number of Titan symbols.

The same ability may be triggered more than once if the symbols cover it. Dice of different colours may be mixed.

The Titan player then returns the spent dice to their owners.

Only the dice the Titan player just gave back may be re-rolled. Dice you kept in step 2 are locked.

Titan faces rolled now are set aside instead of surrendered. This re-roll is free. Always take it.

Resolve the face-up card first. Each action card prints the dice needed to cancel it. If the Heroes pay that cost, nothing happens. If they do not, the effect applies.

The Titan player then reveals the face-down card. The Heroes may cancel it the same way, with whatever dice are left.

Taking a small visible hit to keep dice for the hidden card is a real option. This is the decision the game is built around.

Spend remaining dice in any order: move, deal damage, cycle the tactics deck, or activate the current tactics card.

Damage depends entirely on where the Hero is standing. See the elevation section.

Every player takes back all their dice. Nothing carries over to the next round.

The Titan player sets aside this round's action cards face up, then picks up the cards set aside in the previous round.

On the Titan Tower Ground

The six faces

Every Hero die has the same six faces. One of them works against you.

Titan
the skull

Hand this die to the Titan player at once. They spend it in step 3 to power an ability.

Exception: a Titan face rolled in step 4 is set aside, not surrendered.

Move

Move one Hero up or down one level. Two shortcuts exist: ground to tower first floor, and tower first floor to tower second floor.

Fight

Deal 1 damage. The Hero must be standing on the Titan.

Cannon

Deal 2 damage. The Hero must be standing on the tower. How many cannons are left on your floor does not matter.

Shield
tactics symbol

Two uses. Cancel a Titan action, or send the top tactics card to the bottom of the deck to reach the next one.

Hard cap: the shields all Heroes spend in one round cannot exceed the number of cannons still standing.

Dodge

Avoid an attack aimed at your level. Two dodges are needed. Only a Hero on a targeted level may spend them, and only for themselves.

Shields defend the group. Dodges defend one person. Do not mix them up.

Titan abilities

Your Titan card lists three of these, each with its own symbol cost. This is what the dice the Heroes hand over can buy.

Regeneration X
recover

Move the health marker back X spaces. Undoes Hero damage directly.

Devour X
attack the city

Remove X citizen tokens from play. Twelve exist.

Crush X Cannons
attack the city

You choose which cannons go. This also shrinks the Heroes' shield cap for every round that follows.

Hit Hero
attack a Hero

One Hero loses a heart. The Hero players choose who.

Crush Heroes
attack a Hero

One Hero loses a heart. You choose who. Aim at whoever is already low.

Obstruction
deny

Pick one Hero die. Its owner sets it aside for the rest of the round.

Sudden Change
rewrite

Swap one of your two played action cards for another from hand. Face down stays face down. Face up stays face up. This lets you punish a read.

Frenzy
escalate

Add a third action card, face up. The Heroes now have three threats to pay for from one pool of dice.

Tactics cards

Seven cards in a fixed stack. Only the top one is available. Each sets three conditions: a Hero in one position, a Hero in another, and a symbol payment from the group. Meet all three in one round to trigger it, then remove it from the game.

Neck Slash
ends the game

The Heroes win. Needs the health marker inside the Kill Zone.

Eradication
ends the game

The Heroes win. Also needs the Kill Zone.

Fall Over
huge swing

The Titan takes maximum damage at once. The marker jumps to the far end of the track.

Blinding Shot
information

For the rest of the game the Titan plays both action cards face up. The hidden-card bet disappears.

Mutilation
attrition

Remove one action card from the Titan's hand at random. It is gone for the game.

Healing Cover
repair

Every Hero regains 1 heart, up to a maximum of 3.

Rescue
repair

Return up to 4 citizen tokens to the Titan's feet.

  • You cannot reorder the deck. A shield sends the top card to the bottom. That is a queue, not a search.
  • Digging costs you defence. Every shield spent hunting is a shield you cannot spend cancelling an action this round.
  • Some group payments scale with player count. Fewer Heroes means a smaller payment. Read the number printed on the card in front of you.

The eight Heroes

Each Hero breaks one rule. Pick a team that covers different problems, not the same one twice.

Eren
redistribute

After rolling, give one die to each teammate. Take them back at the end of the round.

Sasha
substitute

Her dodge results count as any symbol she needs.

Erwin
substitute

He may use cannon results as fight results, and fight results as cannon results. He gets round the tower rule.

Mikasa
amplify

Each of her results of one specific type counts double. Check the icon on her card.

Conny
amplify

Each of his results of one specific type counts double. Check the icon on his card.

Levi
deny the Titan

He never surrenders his Titan results. He also cannot re-roll them.

Armin
search

For one shield, pull any tactics card to the top of the deck without disturbing the rest of the order. This is the strongest ability in the box.

Hange
ignore position

She fires cannon results without standing on the tower.

Two players

The box supports 1v1 directly. It is a listed variant, not a fan patch.

The duel

One Titan · one player running two Heroes

  • One player takes the Titan. The other picks two Heroes and controls both.
  • Tactics cards get easier. With two Heroes on the table you meet only 2 of the 3 conditions on a tactics card.
  • The two win cards keep their teeth. Neck Slash and Eradication still demand the Kill Zone and a Hero on level 8.
  • Group payments drop. Collective symbol costs scale down with the number of Heroes.

What survives: the hidden-card guess, the re-roll trade, and the shrinking shield cap. All three work fine at two. What you lose: the argument between Heroes about who spends what. If that negotiation is the appeal, 1v1 is the thinner mode.

Easy to miss

The rules that get played wrong on the first night.

  • Hero dice are public. The Titan player sees every result and hears every plan. Discuss carefully, or not at all.
  • Cannons are your defence budget, not just a clock. The shields the group can spend in a round are capped at the number of cannons left. Losing cannons makes every later round harder to survive.
  • Step 4 only re-rolls returned dice. Dice you locked in during step 2 stay locked.
  • Nothing carries over. Dice reset every round. You cannot bank symbols for a big turn.
  • The Titan's hand is trackable. No action card can appear in back-to-back rounds. Watch the discard.
  • Levels are shared. Several Heroes may stand on one level. That also means one attack can hit all of them.
  • Movement is one step at a time. Level 4 to level 6 costs two moves, because it passes through level 5.
  • Dodges do not help your teammate. Only the Hero on the targeted level can spend them, and only for themselves.
